What a senior move manager does

Someone to run the move, so the family can be family.

What the job is

A senior move manager helps older adults and their families plan and carry out a move: sorting and downsizing, floor-plan layouts, coordinating movers, unpacking and setting up the new home, and often selling or donating what is left. The National Association of Senior & Specialty Move Managers (NASMM) is the trade association; it was called the National Association of Senior Move Managers until 2020.

What NASMM says about its members

  • NASMM states that Senior Move Manager® is a registered service mark it owns, and that only members may use the title.
  • Its FAQ says members are reviewed for insurance and must take its Cornerstone courses, which cover topics such as ethics and safety.
  • There is no legal requirement in Colorado, as far as we could find, to be a NASMM member to offer this kind of service. Membership is a voluntary standard.

Sources: NASMM FAQs; NASMM history.

Questions to ask a move manager

  1. Are you a current NASMM member? Ask them to show it on NASMM’s own directory.
  2. What insurance do you carry, and may I see the certificate?
  3. How do you charge: hourly, by the project, or a percentage of sales?
  4. Who will actually come to the house, and have they done a move like this?
  5. Which moving company do you work with, and do you receive any payment from it?
  6. Can you give me three references to call?
