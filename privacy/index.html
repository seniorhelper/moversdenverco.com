Privacy policy

What we ask for and what we do with it.

Effective 11 November 2026. This site is operated by Eye To Ad Media, Denver, Colorado. Contact: zach@eyetoad.com.

What we ask for

If you fill in the request form, we get your name and phone number, and your email if you choose to add it. We also get who is moving, the places involved, the type of move and month you hope for, the exact consent wording you agreed to and when you agreed. We do not need medical or financial information; please do not type any into the form.

What happens next

A person at Eye To Ad Media reads it and passes it to up to three Denver-area moving companies we have vetted, so they can call you. They are separate businesses with their own privacy practices. We do not sell your details.

This website

It does not use advertising trackers, analytics or outside fonts.

Keeping and deleting

We keep requests only as long as is needed to provide the service and answer questions about consent. You can ask us to delete yours at any time by emailing zach@eyetoad.com. Reply STOP to any text to end texts.

Your rights

Colorado residents may have rights to access, correct and delete personal data under the Colorado Privacy Act. Write to us and we will answer.
